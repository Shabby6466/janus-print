New validators are built from a weighted-sum-mod-N checksum (the shape behind Luhn, NHS numbers, and most national ID or account-number check digits) or an entropy threshold for secrets. The six protected algorithms below cover cases too irregular to reduce to that form and cannot be edited from here.
